# Privacy Policy

DracoViLoc is a personal robotics research project. It uses the Google
Drive API, via an application named `dracoviloc-rclone`/`dracoviloc-sync`,
solely to back up recordings (video and audio captured by the project's own
hardware) from the researcher's own machine to the researcher's own Google
Drive account.

- The application only reads and writes files within the Google Drive
  account of the person who authorizes it. It does not access, read, or
  share any other user's data.
- No data collected by this application is sold, shared, or transferred to
  any third party, advertiser, or external service.
- No data is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than
  storing the researcher's own recordings as a private backup.
- Access can be revoked at any time from
  [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact: see the repository owner at
https://github.com/DhiaNeifar/DracoViLoc.
